# Sunrise clinical pathways (example, for decision support only)

Chest pain with ST elevation: activate the cardiac pathway, notify the cath lab, admit to Cardiac or ICU.

Suspected sepsis: start the sepsis bundle within one hour, admit to ICU or HDU.

Paediatric fever without red flags: outpatient review within 24 hours, advise fluids and fever care.

Every triage suggestion is reviewed and signed by the duty clinician before any action.
