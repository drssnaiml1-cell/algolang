# Outcome-based education guide (example)

Every course outcome (CO) must map to at least one programme outcome (PO) with a strength of 1, 2 or 3.

Course outcomes should use Bloom's taxonomy verbs: remember, understand, apply, analyse, evaluate, create.

NBA requires attainment of each CO to be measured through direct (exams, assignments) and indirect (surveys) tools.

A question paper should cover every unit and at least three Bloom levels.
