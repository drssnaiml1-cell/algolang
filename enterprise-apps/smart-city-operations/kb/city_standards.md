# City operations standards (example municipal corporation, synthetic)

## Air quality: National AQI (CPCB)
- Categories: Good 0-50, Satisfactory 51-100, Moderately polluted 101-200, Poor 201-300, Very poor 301-400, Severe 401-500.
- Very poor: advise people with heart or lung disease, older adults and children to avoid outdoor exertion; step up road dust control and water sprinkling.
- Severe: issue a public health advisory in English, Telugu, Hindi and Urdu; close outdoor school activities; stop construction dust sources; enforce the city's graded response plan.
- Every public advisory is approved by the duty officer before it is published, and says it was drafted with AI assistance.

## Waste collection
- Bins report their fill level; a bin at 75% or more is collected in the next shift.
- Trucks carry about 90 bins' worth of waste; routes start and end at the depot.
- Overflowing bins near markets and hospitals are collected first.

## Electricity distribution
- Feeder limit 60 MW. A forecast peak within 3% of the limit is an overload risk.
- Overload response, in order: demand response from large consumers, transfer load to the neighbouring feeder, then rotational load shedding of non-essential feeders. Hospitals, water pumping and traffic signals are never shed.
- A load-shedding plan needs approval from two officers (the duty engineer and the superintending engineer).
