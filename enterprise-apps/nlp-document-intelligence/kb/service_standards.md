# Customer message handling

- Fraud reports: freeze the affected channel (card, UPI, net banking) within 15 minutes, open a case, and tell the
  customer the case number. Under RBI's customer-liability rules, a customer who reports unauthorised electronic
  transactions within 3 working days bears no loss in most cases; record the time of the report.
- Card block requests: block at once, then offer a replacement.
- Statement and certificate requests: send within one working day through the registered email.
- Service complaints: acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 7 days, or escalate to the nodal officer.
- Never ask a customer for an OTP, PIN or password.
