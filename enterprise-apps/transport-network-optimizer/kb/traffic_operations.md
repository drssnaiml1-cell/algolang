# Traffic and transit operations manual (example city transport authority, synthetic)

## Incident response
- Major delay (3+ minutes added on the best route): publish a diversion within 10 minutes on variable message signs, the city app and radio; deploy traffic police to the first three oversaturated junctions on the detour.
- Oversaturated junctions (flow ratio Y of 0.9 or more): signal timing alone cannot clear the queue. Use police control, a temporary ban on right turns, and meter traffic entering the detour upstream.
- Any police deployment or turn ban is approved by the duty traffic control officer.
- Minor delay: publish the diversion only; retime the detour signals.

## Signal timing
- Webster's method: optimal cycle C0 = (1.5L + 5) / (1 - Y), with L the total lost time; cycles kept between 40 and 150 seconds.
- Green time is split in proportion to each phase's critical flow ratio.

## Bus service planning
- Plan for 85% of a bus's 70-passenger capacity in the peak direction; headways between 3 and 20 minutes.
- A fleet shortfall is covered, in order, by: rescheduling depot maintenance, hiring private buses under the standby contract, and short-turning the busiest routes. Hiring buses needs approval from two officers (depot manager and the transport commissioner's office).
