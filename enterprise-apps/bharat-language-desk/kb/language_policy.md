# Language access policy

- Every citizen is answered in the language they wrote in. Supported: Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali,
  Kannada, Gujarati, English and Hindi written in Latin letters.
- Replies use templates approved by native-speaking officers; free-form machine translation is not sent to
  citizens without review.
- A query whose need cannot be identified is read by an officer, in the original script and in transliteration.
- Language and intent accuracy are measured every month on a multilingual test set; a language below 95% is
  reviewed and its lexicon extended.
