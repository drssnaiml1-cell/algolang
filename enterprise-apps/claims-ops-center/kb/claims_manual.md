# NovaShield claims manual (example)

Motor claims under INR 50,000 with photos and a garage quote can be settled by the fast-track desk within 48 hours.

Claims on a lapsed policy are declined unless the premium was paid within the 30-day grace period.

Two claims on the same policy within 30 days are referred to the Special Investigations Unit (SIU) for review.

Water damage claims need the plumber's report and photos of the source of the leak.

Every decision to decline or refer a claim is reviewed by a licensed claims officer before the customer is told.
