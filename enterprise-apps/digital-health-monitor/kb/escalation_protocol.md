# Home-monitoring escalation protocol (example)

- NEWS2 7 or more: emergency assessment by a clinical team now; consider transfer to hospital.
- NEWS2 5-6, or 3 in any single parameter: urgent review by a clinician within the hour.
- NEWS2 1-4: nurse review; readings every 4-6 hours. NEWS2 0: routine monitoring.
- A major medicine interaction (for example warfarin with aspirin) needs a clinician or pharmacist review the
  same day, whatever the score.
- The system supports clinicians; it never changes treatment. Every alert is reviewed by a person, and the
  patient's background (religion, caste, income) is never used.
