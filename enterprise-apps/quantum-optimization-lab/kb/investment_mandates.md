# AlgoProfessor Quantum Lab — investment mandates (synthetic example)

Balanced growth: four to five large-cap assets, at most two from the same sector, target volatility below 20%.
Diversification across at least three sectors is required for every mandate.

Capital preservation: three large-cap assets with the lowest volatility; utilities, consumer and healthcare preferred.
Every allocation is a PAPER proposal until a named portfolio manager approves it.

Aggressive growth: mid-caps allowed, but only after the risk committee raises the qubit and position limits.
Any quantum optimisation result must be VERIFIED against the exact classical optimum before it is used.
