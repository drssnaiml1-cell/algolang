# Pump maintenance standards (example)

- Severity zones for vibration velocity (RMS, ISO 10816-3 style for medium machines): A below 1.4 mm/s is good,
  B below 2.8 acceptable, C below 4.5 needs a planned repair, D needs action now.
- A pump with predicted failure within 4 weeks (lower end of the 90% interval) gets an urgent work order; within
  12 weeks, a planned repair at the next shutdown.
- Bearing outer-race faults: replace the bearing and check lubrication; misalignment: laser-align coupling;
  imbalance: balance the impeller.
- Isolation and lock-out/tag-out before any work. A person approves every urgent work order; two managers approve
  the weekly crew schedule.
